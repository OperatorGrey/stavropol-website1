Folk description of Stavropol

Stavropol is a city where nothing ever happens. Wars, floods, hurricanes, and terrorist attacks occur right next door—and while the neighboring town of Nowhereville appears in the news, Stavropol does not. The only exception was when they tore down the saber at the intersection of Shpakovskaya and Dovatortsev Streets (a local source of traffic congestion), which demonstrates “incredible” interest in this city. It also symbolizes that outside the Southern Federal District and the North Caucasus Federal District, at most 0.05% of the population has ever heard of this city, even while being ranked among the top fifty in terms of population and a host of other metrics.

-Anonymous
